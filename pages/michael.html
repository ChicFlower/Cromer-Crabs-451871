Michael
The Deafened Destroyer
This is Michael.

He fights so that everything is safe and sound.

Except the latter.

He has an amazing sense of direction when playing and is perfect at communicating; his headset is built in so he is always ready to game, and he likes to make is friends & husbands proud!

He loves a challenge and loves to do what he is told he shouldn't be doing!

They told him not to stare at the sun cause it would hurt his eyes.

It didn't hurt. Anyways he has pretty much permeant light sensitivity now - they do say lose one sense and gain another!

Although one pro is that rather than his body putting effort into making sure he can hear, it made him taller.

Now thats something to be proud of.
